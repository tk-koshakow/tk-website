## Pitch 1: Entity Grounding & The 4 Surfaces of Truth (Why Schema Catfishing Fails in AI Search)

- **Article & Author:** *[Schema For AI Citations: How To Become A Trusted Source](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/schema-ai-citations-trusted-source/586785/)* by **Loren Baker** (Founder, *Search Engine Journal*, Sept 1, 2026)
- **Author LinkedIn:** [Loren Baker](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lorenbaker/)
- **Core Thesis:** Schema markup doesn't create trust; it makes trust *machine-verifiable*. LLMs and search engines validate entities across 4 surfaces: (1) The visible webpage, (2) JSON-LD schema, (3) Platform of record (Google Business Profile, Merchant Center), and (4) Third-party corroboration (reviews, directories, publications). Inconsistencies act like "entity catfishing" and cause AI engines to drop citation eligibility.
- **TK's Angle (The Take):** Loren Baker nailed the exact difference between 2016 SEO and 2026 AEO. In classic SEO, you could stuff structured data with flattering claims and hope Google parsed it. In AI search, LLMs perform real-time cross-surface reconciliation. If your JSON-LD claims your software is the "leading enterprise CRM" but community discourse on Reddit and G2 highlights buggy migrations, the LLM catches the hallucination immediately. Structured data is an integrity test, not a spin machine.
