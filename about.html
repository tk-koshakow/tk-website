About TK

Hi, I’m TK.

I currently lead SEO/AEO strategy for an enterprise industrial software company. I’ve been in the SEO/AEO industry for just about a decade, with over 5 of those years spent at a boutique agency specializing in B2B tech, where I was the Strategy Director. We had clients like Adobe, Lyft, Airtable, Skedulo, Atlassian and many more.

Before that I worked in publishing, both literary and academic. I also used to write quite a bit. Here are a couple of examples:

  • Fiction: The Moment 19 — A short story published in Necessary Fiction, 2013
  • Non-fiction: Wow and Flutter #7: Spirit of Elijah — Part of an essay series I wrote for Hobart

The point of this site is for me to learn. The best way to do that is to ask good questions (for example, how do I build a site that gets a perfect score on PageSpeed Insights?), try to answer them, and then write about it.

Also, I really like mushrooms. I live in Madison, WI. I’m a husband, dad, and bicycle enthusiast.
